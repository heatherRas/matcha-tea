How do you make matcha the right way?

Step 1: Good quality Matcha powder is essential for a good tasting matcha. Add 1 - 2 teaspoons of sifted matcha to a clean, dry chawan or small bowl.

Step 2: Hot Water heated between 175 F to 185 F (75 C to 85 C). Make sure water is not boiling as it will scorch the powder and taste bitter. Add in a small amount to create a concentrated matcha

Step 3: Using a bamboo whisk (soaked) whisk back and forth in a zig zag motion. Continue for 30-45 seconds until a foamy layer forms on top.

Step 4: For a traditional matcha, add the remainder of hot water. For a matcha latte, pour the concentrate over heated milk.

Enjoy!
